# Ronin Bridge, 2022-03-23

**Loss:** about $624M (173,600 ETH and 25.5M USDC) · **Design:** lock-mint, 5-of-9 validator multisig · **Root cause:** key/signer compromise · **Chains:** Ethereum, Ronin · **Status:** confirmed

## Summary
An attacker controlled 5 of the 9 validator keys: 4 Sky Mavis keys, plus an Axie DAO signing allowlist that had never been revoked. With that quorum, two validly signed withdrawals emptied nearly the whole Ethereum escrow in under two minutes. Nobody noticed for six days.

## What failed
Key/signer compromise: the attacker reached the signing threshold. The contract behaved exactly as designed.

## On-chain signature
- Two withdrawals, each signed by a valid quorum, sent to an address funded 12 minutes earlier.
- The ETH withdrawal was near-total of the escrow's ETH balance.

## BridgeWatch rules that would fire
- `large_withdrawal`: on the first transaction (block 14442835). 173,600 ETH is many times the escrow's normal daily outflow.
- `escrow_drain`: on the same block. The ETH balance dropped by more than 90%.
- `outflow_spike`: on the same block.
- Latency: one block (~13 s) plus pipeline latency. Any alert that a human read within the 6-day gap would have mattered. Deposits kept arriving into a drained bridge during that time.

## False-alarm considerations
- No legitimate withdrawal of this size had ever happened, and the recipient had no deposit history.
- The Forta alerts show the real risk is alerts nobody reads. Forta described its own feed as "fairly noisy overall". Precision matters more than coverage.

## Response and outcome
- Bridge halted on discovery, and the validator threshold was raised to 8 of 9.
- Sky Mavis reimbursed users from a funding round.
- About $30M was later seized by authorities (Chainalysis). The rest was not recovered.
